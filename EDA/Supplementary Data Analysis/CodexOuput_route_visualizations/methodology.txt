Normalization: raw official input only. Left-moving plays rotate x=120-x, y=53.3-y. Translate first receiver point to (0,0). Mirror lateral displacement by initial side of field so positive means toward that initial sideline. Longitudinal axis is downfield; no rescaling of yards. Median trajectories interpolate every path to 61 equally spaced fractions of its own observed duration, then take coordinatewise medians; these are not event-aligned or fixed-clock trajectories. Seed 20261025 samples at most 250 paths per class, while medians and statistics use all matched paths. Heuristic break: Gaussian smoothing sigma=1 frame; headings over 0.3s before/after a candidate, both vector lengths >=0.6 yd; choose largest absolute turn >=35 degrees and >=0.3s after window start. Signed angle positive=outward. No qualifying turn produces missing break features. This is an observed-turn proxy, not a validated route-break detector. Max speed uses supplied s in yd/s. Separation uses raw Euclidean distance to all supplied defenders at the target final input frame; missing defender sets yield missing values. DB subset: CB/DB/FS/SS/S. Depth bands use maximum observed displacement, not line-of-scrimmage depth. Density peaks are exploratory scipy gaussian_kde peaks (12% prominence; minimum grid separation 25).
